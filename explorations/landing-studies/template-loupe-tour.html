A loupe over moving pixels.

Columns of 16 px icons drift up at true 1× pixels, one whole pixel at a time. The loupe floats between them, stops over a column while icons pass under it, and moves on. The number is the ink coverage of the pixel under the loupe's center, read from the screen as it moves.

Coverage is the alpha of the icon ink at one screen pixel when your browser draws the icon at 16 px: 100% is a solid pixel and 0% is empty. The columns move in whole pixels, so every icon stays as sharp as a still one. Values can vary slightly by browser.
