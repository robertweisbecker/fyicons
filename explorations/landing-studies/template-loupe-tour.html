Five pixels, up close.

A loupe moves along a toolbar drawn at true 1× pixels and stops on one pixel in five icons. The large number is the ink coverage of the pixel under the loupe, read from the screen raster as it moves.

Toolbar · 16 px icons at 1×
–ink coverage

Coverage is the alpha of the icon ink at one pixel when your browser draws the icon at 16 px. 100% is a solid pixel and 0% is empty. Values can vary slightly by browser.
