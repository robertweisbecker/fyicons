3.1Construction · optical sizes

Size the glyph. Keep the line.

Some controls ship a -sm or -lg drawing on the same 16 px canvas. The glyph gets smaller or larger, and the line stays at the weight of the set. Scaling the regular icon instead changes the line with it.

In 13 px interface text

Each control shows the same three choices. Scaled sizes come from matching the glyph size of the optical drawing, rounded to whole pixels.

3.2Construction · modifiers

Four kinds of modifier.

A variant adds meaning to a base icon. The page compares each variant with its base, pixel by pixel, to find where the new ink lands and how much of the base it cuts away.

Each dot is the center of the new ink in one variant. Select a dot to name it.

In the four drawings: blue is new ink, pink is ink cut from the base, and gray is shared.

What the names mean

Measured kind for each suffix. Exceptions are variants whose new ink lands somewhere else.
