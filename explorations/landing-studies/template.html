Part 2 · Drawing style

“Constructed with care” today shows four keylines. These studies make the drawing rules specific, then show that the rules hold across the set.

2.1 · Opener for “Constructed with care”

Anatomy of one icon

One familiar icon, measured. Every value comes from the shipped path data of envelope.

01
16 × 16 canvas

Drawn at the size it ships. No downscaled 24 px artwork.

viewBox 0 0 16 16
02
1 px walls on whole pixels

Outer edge at x 1, inner edge at x 2. Each wall fills one pixel column.

1 → 2, 14 → 15
03
Concentric corners

Outer radius 2, inner radius 1. The wall stays 1 px through the curve.

r 2 / r 1
04
Fits a keyline

Wide shapes use the 14 × 10 horizontal keyline, so they carry the same weight as round and tall icons.

14 × 10
05
Filled outlines

Strokes are expanded to filled paths, so the icon looks the same in every renderer and in currentColor.

2.4 · Backs up “pixel-perfect” in the hero

Pixel proof

The same icon rasterized at 16 px by your browser, on the grid and nudged by half a pixel. Soft pixels are partly covered pixels, which read as blur.

Actual size

2.5 · Small add-on to 2.4

Integer scale ladder

Drawn at 16, the icons stay sharp at whole multiples. Use 16, 32, and 48 for the cleanest results; 20 and 24 work for touch targets.
