02Specimen loupe · for “Drawn pixel-perfect”

03Comparison · real SVGs from each package

Six sets at 16 px, the size dense UI uses.

The same 14 controls, rasterized in your browser. Lucide, Remix, and Hugeicons are drawn for 24 px and go soft at 16. Phosphor and Font Awesome scale down to 16 cleanly, as FYIcons does. Pixel alignment is the floor. Studies 04 to 06 cover what sets the drawing apart.

Hover to read each size.

Each set is sharpest at its own grid

Edge blur counts partly covered pixels per pixel of outline, summed over the 14 controls, so filled and outlined styles compare fairly. Lower is sharper. A curve dips where its drawing grid lines up with the pixel grid: 24 for Lucide and Remix, 16 and 32 for FYIcons, Phosphor, and Font Awesome. Diagonals, like the chevron and close icons, can never be fully crisp in any set.

04Comparison · house style vs. range

Quiet controls. Loud creatures.

Most sets apply one house style to everything, so a cat is drawn with the same rules as a chevron. FYIcons changes its voice by subject: plain where you click, characterful where you look.

05Breakdown · optical centering

The play button is not centered. On purpose.

A triangle centered by its box looks too far left, because most of its weight sits near the flat edge. Step through the fix, then compare each set's play icon, measured from its raster.

06Breakdown · keyline sizes

Why the circle gets 14 px and the square gets 12.

Equal boxes don't look equal. A circle loses its corners, so it must be wider to carry the same weight. Drag the circle and watch the areas.
